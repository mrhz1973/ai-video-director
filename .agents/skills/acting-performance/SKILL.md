# Acting performance

1. Read references/ACTING_SYSTEM.md completely before writing or judging an acting layer.
2. Read the active shot, continuity bible and latest accepted/rejected review.
3. Define a playable objective, obstacle, stakes and tactic. Do not direct an emotion as the action.
4. Convert the inner event into camera-readable behavior: gaze, blink, breath, contact, posture, timing and tactic changes.
5. Fit two to four visible beats to the available duration without overloading the shot.
6. Make thought appear in the eyes before speech or head movement when the scene calls for it.
7. Preserve physical contact, weight, listening and a continuous life before and after the scripted beat.
8. Review the result against the manual's performance scale and pre-send checklist.
9. Deliver only the acting layer or the requested critique unless the user asks for full prompt integration.
