# Audio plan

## Principle

Higgsfield outputs remain silent. The final edit restores the exact external source audio rather than synthesizing dialogue, radio, music or effects.

## Workflow

1. Analyze the source audio and picture timing locally.
2. Record exact shot boundaries and any deliberate visual overlap.
3. Generate and review silent shots.
4. Assemble approved picture to the source duration.
5. Conform frame rate and retime only within approved tolerances.
6. Mux or place the untouched source audio against the final picture.
7. Verify sync at every shot boundary and at the final frame.
8. Export a review copy and an archival master externally.

## Repository boundary

Do not commit the source audio, extracted WAV, final mux or edit project. Record only neutral filenames, duration, technical metadata and SHA-256 after the final artifacts exist.
