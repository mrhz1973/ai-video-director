# ADR-003 — V3 generation evaluation

Date: 2026-08-17
Generation: V3 / 1c(1).mp4
Status: partially approved, not final

## Approved

- facial identity and realism;
- framing and camera placement;
- radio-hut composition;
- restrained performance;
- eye movement preceding the final head turn.

## Rejected

- tattoo is larger, higher and visually redesigned;
- chest and arms contain artificial body hair;
- head is too large relative to torso;
- shoulders and body scale are reduced.

## Prompt contribution to failure

The V3 prompt used "body hair" as a preservation target and named the tattoo as a "winged-rose tattoo." These textual descriptions competed with the real reference and authorized reinterpretation.

## Final assessment

V3 is a face, framing and performance reference only. It must not become the body or tattoo continuity master.
